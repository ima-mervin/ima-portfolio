About

I'm a Business Analyst at Denver Public Schools in the Department of Technology Services, where I work on product and process: documenting and assessing business processes, building data governance and standards, reviewing system access, and automating compliance data work. I have a specialization in Six Sigma Black Belt from Kennesaw State University and hold the NIST Cybersecurity Framework certification, which I apply to process improvement and governance. Before DPS, I was an Information Technology Consultant at CU Boulder's Office of Information Technology, running access reviews and security awareness training. I completed a bachelor's in computer science and a master's in business analytics at CU Boulder, and my projects are full-stack and machine learning builds with security designed in. My interest lies in product, process, and engineering.
